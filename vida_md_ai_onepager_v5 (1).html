Section 1
Two roles, one AI practice
VIDA already has foundational AI infrastructure in place through the Operations role. This proposal builds on that foundation — not over it. The two roles are designed to be complementary, not redundant.
⚙
Operations role
AI operations (current)
Manages AI tool subscriptions and vendor relationships
Maintains internal AI governance documentation and policies
Oversees compliance with internal AI standards
Handles tool approval and access administration
Supports operational continuity of AI infrastructure
+
▲
MD, AI Strategy & Impact
Proposed
Sets VIDA's AI strategic direction internally and externally
Builds and maintains the internal AI tool library and hub
Leads AI integration on client engagements and proposals
Owns the external AI pipeline — partnerships, RFPs, thought leadership
Builds staff AI fluency and capacity across the team
How the two roles work together
→ Operations sets the guardrails. AI Strategy & Impact builds within them.
→ New tools proposed by AI Strategy go through Operations for approval and documentation.
→ AI Strategy flags governance gaps; Operations updates policy to address them.
→ Together they create a complete AI function — one holding infrastructure, one driving growth.
Section 2
How this differs from the existing MD, Strategy & Impact role
VIDA's current MD, Strategy & Impact role owns organizational strategy, partnership development, and delivery quality across the full portfolio. The AI-focused MD role is a specialization within that same framework — not a duplication of it. Below is a direct mapping of what this role owns in practice.
▪
Direction & delivery
✓ Own VIDA's AI strategic framework as a living document the whole team operates from
✓ Serve as the strategic AI advisor on any VIDA engagement involving AI integration, adoption, or governance
✓ Set and maintain the standard for what rigorous, ethical AI engagement looks like at VIDA
Vertical depth in one practice area — not horizontal strategy across all of VIDA.
▪
Continued data lead duties
✓ Remains data lead on assigned projects — dashboards, data deliverables, program data management
✓ Data governance work done here feeds directly into AI infrastructure development
✓ Improving how we store and structure data unlocks better AI tools for the whole team
The data lead role is preserved — AI strategy is additive, not a replacement.
▪
Team AI capacity & culture
✓ Design and facilitate a formal AI fluency pathway for VIDA staff
✓ Build tools and resources the team can use independently without escalating every decision
✓ Embed community-centered AI practice as an onboarding touchpoint and team norm
The current MD, S&I leads team development broadly. This role leads AI fluency specifically.
▪
Growth, partnerships & brand
✓ Lead from the front on AI-specific proposals and pursuit strategy — not in a supporting role
✓ Own VIDA's AI-specific partnerships — partner firms, co-design relationships, AI community network
✓ Advance VIDA's external AI presence through thought leadership and case studies
The current MD, S&I owns growth broadly. This role owns the AI growth lane specifically.
Section 3
How we operationalize this shift
Three parallel workstreams, grounded in goals already established — beginning with a capacity alignment conversation and moving into internal and external AI strategy execution.
1
Align on capacity with the data team
A structured conversation to map current duties, identify what shifts, and carve out the capacity needed to carry this role without overextending.
What this conversation covers
Review current project assignments and data lead responsibilities
Identify which duties can be absorbed, delegated, or phased down
Define protected time for AI strategy work — internal and external
Establish a transition timeline that does not disrupt active engagements
2
Goal 1 — Data lead (maintained)
Data lead responsibilities on assigned projects — dashboards, data deliverables, and program data management — remain part of the role. Data governance work done here feeds directly into AI governance infrastructure.

3
Goal 2 — Internal AI strategy
Build the internal infrastructure that makes VIDA's AI practice consistent, accessible, and durable for the whole team.
Internal
Build and launch the VIDA AI Hub
A centralized internal platform where staff can access approved tools, submit feedback on tools in progress, request new builds, and get answers about AI policy — without digging through documentation. A working prototype has already been built and is ready to be hosted.

View the working prototype ↗
Data governance → AI governance
Reorganize how VIDA stores and structures internal data so AI tools can actually be built on top of it. One of the clearest gaps is how past proposals are stored — currently they live in silos that make it difficult to surface, reuse, or build AI-assisted workflows around them. Improved data structure means better tools, built faster, for the whole team.
4
Goal 3 — External AI strategy
Build VIDA's external AI pipeline through a staged approach — starting with free tools that create visibility, then building toward funded engagements and thought leadership.
External
Pipeline stage 1 — free reflection tools
Create AI readiness and reflection tools that organizations can use at no cost. These build trust, generate visibility, and position VIDA as a credible, accessible entry point into AI strategy for mission-driven organizations.

Example: Know Your Soil — free reflection tool ↗
Pipeline stage 2 — case studies
Document case studies from internal and external AI activities — the tools we've built, the engagements we've led, and the governance frameworks we've developed. These become the evidence base for future proposals and thought leadership.
Pipeline stage 3 — thought leadership
Continue publishing white papers on AI governance and equity in the social sector
Seek opportunities to co-create with community partners and publish case studies publicly under VIDA's brand
Pipeline stage 4 — RFP tracking
Build an AI pipeline tracking system for open and closed RFPs in the AI integration and capacity-building space.
Additional input needed — Larkin & leadership team
Pipeline stage 5 — AI cohort for nonprofits
Research and pursue opportunities to build or participate in AI learning cohorts designed specifically for nonprofit organizations — similar to models like Bellwether's AI Cohorts. This positions VIDA as a capacity-building partner, not just a consulting firm.
